投投乐 TouTouLe Founder & product owner, solo发起人兼产品负责人（独立完成） 2026.09 — Python · Claude API · Pydantic · Streamlit · SQLite

An AI agent that triages job postings with evidence带证据的招聘信息筛选 AI Agent

A scheduled prompt gives you a summary you have to trust. TouTouLe gives you a decision record you can verify, with a measured error rate on the fields where an error costs an opportunity.定时提示词只能给你一份需要信任的摘要；投投乐给你一份可以核验的决策记录，并且在“出错就会错失机会”的字段上有实测错误率。

Problem问题
Each posting took 15–20 minutes to check eligibility, application caps, deadline and required materials, and mistakes landed where they cost most.每条 JD 要花 15–20 分钟核对资格、投递上限、截止日期和材料要求，而最容易出错的恰好是代价最高的地方。
My role我的角色
Everything: PRD, decision log, eval design, labeling, code, deployment.全部独立完成：PRD、决策日志、评测设计、标注、开发和上线。
Outcome结果
v0.1.0 live. Critical hallucinations down 60–70% vs. baseline; critical misses held under 10%.v0.1.0 已上线。关键字段幻觉较基线下降 60–70%，关键字段漏检控制在 10% 以内。
  • Evidence or nothing. Every extracted value carries a verbatim quote, and code checks it against the posting. A field that fails becomes “Not stated”.没有证据就不算数。每个抽取值都附原文引用，由代码逐字比对原文；校验失败的字段直接记为“未说明”。
  • Rules decide eligibility, not the model. Six deterministic rules flag visa, graduation window, degree, deadline and cap problems.资格由规则判断，而不是模型。六条确定性规则检查签证、毕业时间、学位、截止日期和投递上限。
  • Evals tiered by the cost of an error. 50 labeled postings, 3 iterations, and a written hypothesis before each run.按错误代价分层评测。50 条标注 JD，3 轮迭代，每轮先写下假设再运行。
  • Say what it doesn’t do. The README lists the targets it missed and why.写清楚它做不到什么。README 列出了没达标的指标和原因。

An AI planner that never acts without your approval一个未经你批准绝不动手的 AI 日程助手

Dayweave turns coursework, calendar and inbox evidence into a reviewable daily plan. It keeps what a source says, what the assistant recommends and what you approve visibly separate.Dayweave 把课程、日历和邮件里的信息整理成一份可审阅的每日计划，并始终把“来源说了什么”“助手建议什么”“你批准了什么”分开展示。

2 · Recommendation2 · 建议

Deterministic focus blocks: 09:00–24:00, at most 90 minutes each, at least 20% of the day left free.确定性的专注时段：09:00–24:00 之间，每段不超过 90 分钟，每天至少留 20% 空闲。

3 · Approval3 · 批准

Nothing is written to a calendar until you approve an exact, versioned request in chat.只有你在对话中批准了某个具体版本的请求，才会写入日历。

  • Stale data looks stale. A failed read keeps the last good records and says so, instead of showing an empty inbox or a clear day.过期数据要看得出过期。读取失败时保留上一次的有效记录并明确标注，而不是显示成“收件箱为空”或“今天没事”。
  • Proposals fail closed. Requests that overlap known events, fall outside verified coverage, sit in the past or reuse an action ID are rejected.拿不准就拒绝。与已知日程冲突、超出已验证范围、时间已过或重复使用操作编号的请求一律拒绝。

About关于我

I start from the cost of being wrong我习惯先想清楚“错了代价是什么”

My background spans economics, consulting, strategy and operations. Across all of it I learned that a confident number is worth little until you know how it was made, and which mistakes would actually cost something.我的背景横跨经济学、咨询、策略和运营。这些经历让我明白，一个看起来很确定的数字，在你知道它怎么来的、错了会付出什么代价之前，没什么价值。

That shapes how I work, whether I am sizing a market, running a campaign or building with AI: get to the decision fast, show the evidence behind it, and say plainly what is still uncertain.无论是做市场分析、推进大促，还是用 AI 做产品，我的方式都一样：尽快推到决策，把背后的证据摆出来，并坦白说明哪些地方还不确定。
