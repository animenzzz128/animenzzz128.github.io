投投乐 TouTouLe Founder & product owner, solo发起人兼产品负责人（独立完成） 2026.09 — Python · Claude API · Pydantic · Streamlit · SQLite

An AI agent that triages job postings with evidence带证据的招聘信息筛选 AI Agent

A scheduled prompt gives you a summary you have to trust. TouTouLe gives you a decision record you can verify, with a measured error rate on the fields where an error costs an opportunity.定时提示词只能给你一份需要信任的摘要；投投乐给你一份可以核验的决策记录，并且在“出错就会错失机会”的字段上有实测错误率。

Problem问题
Each posting took 15–20 minutes to check eligibility, application caps, deadline and required materials, and mistakes landed exactly where they cost most.每条 JD 要花 15–20 分钟核对资格、投递次数上限、截止日期和材料要求，而且最容易出错的恰好是代价最高的地方。
My role我的角色
Everything: PRD, decision log, eval design, labeling, code, deployment.全部独立完成：PRD、决策日志、评测设计、标注、开发和上线。
Outcome结果
v0.1.0 live. Critical hallucinations down 60–70% vs. baseline; critical misses held under 10%.v0.1.0 已上线。关键字段幻觉较基线下降 60–70%，关键字段漏检控制在 10% 以内。
  • Evidence or nothing. Every extracted value carries a verbatim quote, and code checks it against the posting. A field that fails becomes “Not stated”.没有证据就不算数。每个抽取值都附原文引用，由代码逐字比对原文；校验失败的字段直接记为“未说明”。
  • Rules decide eligibility, not the model. Six deterministic rules flag visa, graduation window, degree, deadline and cap problems. A missing field never excludes a job.资格由规则判断，而不是模型。六条确定性规则检查签证、毕业时间、学位、截止日期和投递上限；未说明的字段永远不会把职位排除掉。
  • Evals tiered by the cost of an error. 50 labeled postings, 3 iterations, and a written hypothesis before each run.按错误代价分层评测。50 条标注 JD，3 轮迭代，每轮先写下假设再运行。
  • Say what it doesn’t do. The README lists the targets it missed and why, including a match score that agrees with my own ratings only 3 times in 20.写清楚它做不到什么。README 列出了没达标的指标和原因，包括匹配分只有 3/20 与我本人的打分一致。

About关于我

I start from the cost of being wrong我习惯先想清楚“错了代价是什么”

My background spans economics, consulting, strategy and operations. Across all of it I learned that a confident number is worth little until you know how it was made, and which mistakes would actually cost something.我的背景横跨经济学、咨询、策略和运营。这些经历让我明白，一个看起来很确定的数字，在你知道它怎么来的、错了会付出什么代价之前，没什么价值。
